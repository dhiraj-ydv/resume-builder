# Impact Writer Skill

## Goal

Turn verified evidence into concise, credible resume content that shows ownership, complexity, and outcomes.

## Bullet formula

Use the strongest natural version of:

**Action + what changed/built + important constraint/scale + measurable or observable result.**

Do not force every bullet into the same syntax. Clarity beats formulaic writing.

## Writing rules

- Start with specific actions: designed, shipped, launched, automated, migrated, reduced, improved, defined, built, integrated, scaled, analyzed, or coordinated.
- Name the product/system/domain when it adds signal.
- Make the candidate's contribution clear without inflating ownership.
- Prefer outcomes over responsibilities.
- Keep one main idea per bullet.
- Delete filler such as “responsible for,” “worked on,” “helped with,” “results-driven,” and “dynamic professional.”
- Use numbers only when verified. If a result is qualitative, state the real qualitative impact instead of fabricating a metric.
- Prefer precise technical nouns over stacks of adjectives.

## AI resume writing

For AI-related work, communicate both product and technical judgment. Strong bullets may show:

- why AI was appropriate for the workflow,
- how model outputs were constrained or validated,
- how evaluation or human review was designed,
- how retrieval/context/tool use improved results,
- how latency, reliability, or inference cost was handled,
- what changed for users or operations.

Avoid empty phrases such as “leveraged cutting-edge AI” or “used generative AI to drive innovation.”

## Compression pass

After drafting:

1. Remove repeated context.
2. Replace vague verbs with specific ones.
3. Move the strongest differentiator toward the beginning.
4. Remove technologies that do not strengthen the target-role story.
5. Check that every strong claim can be defended with evidence.
